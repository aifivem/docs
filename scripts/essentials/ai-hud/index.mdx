ai-hud draws the HUD and keeps the character's needs: hunger, thirst and stress. Hunger and thirst drop over time and food and drink fill them up; stress grows from shooting and fast driving and fades by itself.

## What it does in the game

- **Status pills.** Health, armour, hunger, thirst and stress.
- **Money.** Cash and bank, top right.
- **Name and job.** The job (the on-duty job, else the first one) with its grade, the server id and the character name, top right. "Off duty" shows when the player is not on duty.
- **Weapon box.** While a weapon is held: its name, picture and rounds, under the money.
- **Speedometer.** In a vehicle: speed, gear, fuel and the seatbelt warning.
- **Street and area** next to the minimap.
- **Voice.** The microphone, the voice range, talking and the radio (needs ai-voice or pma-voice).
- **Key hints.** On the left, the keys of what the player can do now (on foot or in a vehicle).
- **Minimap.** Always, or only in a vehicle, as the panel says. GTA's own health and armour bars under it are hidden.
- **Hides itself** without a character, in the pause menu, during screen fades and while a full-screen menu is open.
- **Player settings.** `/hud` or the radial menu's **HUD settings** entry: each player turns parts of the HUD off for themselves. A part the panel turns off stays off.

### How the needs work

- Hunger and thirst go from 100 (full) down to 0. Stress goes from 0 (calm) up to 100.
- Every minute of play, hunger and thirst drop and stress fades by the panel's amounts.
- While hunger or thirst is at 0, the character loses health every 10 seconds.
- Shooting and driving fast add stress.
- Food, drink and other items change the needs by their **Hunger**, **Thirst** and **Stress** values in the inventory's item settings ([ai-inventory](/docs/scripts/essentials/ai-inventory)).
- Needs are saved on the character (QB's `hunger`, `thirst`, `stress` metadata keys), so QB scripts read them too.

## Installation

The AI Core txAdmin recipe installs it. By hand: put the folder in `resources/[ai]/` and add `ensure ai-hud` to `server.cfg` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs ai-core. It has no database table of its own.

Run only one HUD. AI Core does not report two HUDs running at once.

## Panel settings

Open the admin panel (**F10**) and pick **HUD**. Everything is on one tab, **HUD and needs**.

**Needs**: hunger and thirst drop over time; food and drink items fill them up.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hunger drop | Points lost every minute from 100. 0.25 empties in about 6.5 hours of play. | 0 to 10 per min | 0.25 |
| Thirst drop | Points lost every minute from 100. | 0 to 10 per min | 0.35 |
| Stress fade | Stress points that fade every minute. | 0 to 10 per min | 0.5 |
| Stress speed | Driving faster than this may add a stress point. 0 turns it off. | 0 to 500 km/h | 160 |
| Speed stress chance | Chance per speed change above the stress speed to add a point. | 0 to 100 % | 1 |
| Stress per shot | Points a shot adds, at most once a second. 0 turns it off. | 0 to 5 | 1 |
| Damage when empty | Health lost every 10 seconds while food or water is at 0. 0 turns it off. | 0 to 50 HP | 2 |

**What the HUD shows**: parts of the HUD players see while playing.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Health and needs | The status pills. | on / off | on |
| Money | Cash and bank. | on / off | on |
| Speedometer | The gauge in vehicles. | on / off | on |
| Street name | Street and area. | on / off | on |
| Voice | Microphone and voice range (needs ai-voice or pma-voice). | on / off | on |
| Name and job | Top right: the job, its grade, the server id and the character name. | on / off | on |
| Speed unit | Unit of the speedometer. | km/h / mph | km/h |

**Map and key hints**: when the minimap shows and the key list on the left of the screen.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimap | **Always**, or **Only in a vehicle**. | Always / Only in a vehicle | Only in a vehicle |
| Key hints | The keys of what the player can do now, on the left of the screen. Which keys show, and when, is set per key in the **Keybinds** tab. | on / off | on |

## Commands

| Command | Arguments | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/hud` | none | everyone | Opens the HUD settings: one switch per HUD part and the key hints, kept for this player. |

## Keybinds

None of its own. The key hints it shows come from every script's keybinds.

## Works with other scripts

- **Fuel** from any fuel script that sets the vehicle's `fuel` state bag (ai-fuel, and others through AI Core's fuel layer).
- **Seatbelt** through AI Core's seatbelt layer (ai-smallresources and other seatbelt scripts).
- **Voice** from ai-voice or pma-voice.
- **Needs from items** of any inventory that defines hunger, thirst or stress values (AI Core's item use event).
- **Stress from other scripts** through AI Core's stress layer (lib `stress`), which calls ai-hud.
- **You can replace it.** AI Core hides and shows other HUDs (tgiann-lumihud, jg-hud, 0r-hud-v3, codem-supreme-hud, qb-hud, ps-hud and more) and opens their settings from the radial menu. See [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility).

## Known limits

- Stress from the client is limited to 1 to 5 points per report and two reports a second; everything else is decided on the server.
- The minimap mode is the owner's choice: players cannot change it in `/hud`. An admin can keep it on foot for themselves from the ai-admin menu.
