ai-appearance gives characters their look. A new character first goes through the creator (face, parents, hair, clothes) alone in a private room. Later, players change clothes in clothing stores and their hair, beard and makeup at barber shops. It also makes the round portraits on AI Core's character screen.

It is basic on purpose: creator, clothing stores and barbers. It has no tattoos, saved outfits, wardrobe, clothing items or job outfit menu. For those, install a fuller clothing script (see below).

## What it does in the game

- **Character creator.** A new character appears in a private copy of the world (nobody else sees them), at the creator spot you set, and shapes their look: parents (mother and father drive face shape and skin), face features (on 2D planes like GTA's own creator, double click resets), hair and colours, beard, brows, makeup, and clothes. Saving brings them to their spawn point. A character without a saved look goes back to the creator at the next login.
- **Clothing stores.** Inside a store's area, the interaction key opens the editor for clothes and accessories. The player pays per changed item.
- **Barber shops.** Hair, hair colours, beard, brows, chest hair, makeup, blush and lipstick. The player pays once per visit when anything changed.
- **Free when nothing changed.** Closing without a change costs nothing.
- **Portraits.** The character screen shows each character's face on its card.
- **Freemode models only.** The model follows the character's sex chosen at creation; the creator never changes it.
- Every save is checked on the server: the creator only for a character without a look, a shop only inside that shop and only for the parts it sells.

## Installation

The AI Core txAdmin recipe installs it. By hand: put the folder in `resources/[ai]/` and add `ensure ai-appearance` to `server.cfg` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs ai-core and the database:

```ini title="env.cfg"
add_convar_permission ai-appearance read mysql_connection_string
```

The table `ai_appearance_characters` is created on the first start.

## Panel settings

Open the admin panel (**F10**) and pick **Appearance**.

### Clothing stores and barbers tab

Where players change clothes or get a haircut. Each store is one record; press **New store** to add one.

**Store**: stand in the middle of the store and use your position; players inside the radius can open it.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default for a new store |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | Clothing store or barber shop. | Clothing store / Barber shop | Clothing store |
| Centre of the store | The middle of the store. Also where the map blip sits. | a point | |
| Radius | How far from the centre the store can be opened. | 1 to 30 m | 6 |
| Show on the map | Shows the store's blip. | on / off | on |

**NPC**: a person standing at this place (a clerk). Leave the model empty for none.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model | The ped model; empty means no NPC. Players see it within 50 m. | a ped model | empty |
| What it does | An idle animation the NPC plays on the spot. | a list of scenarios | none |
| Where it stands | The NPC's floor spot and the way it faces. Without one it stands on the store's point. | a point with heading | |

The script ships with 16 clothing stores and 7 barber shops at the GTA locations.

### Appearance settings tab

**Character creator**: new characters shape their look here, alone in a private copy of the world.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Where the character stands | Stand on the spot facing the way the character should face, then use your position. | a point with heading | -795.57, 331.79, 200.42, heading 270 |

**Prices**: what a visit costs. Nothing is charged when the player changes nothing.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clothing, per changed item | Price of each changed piece. A new top, pants and shoes is three items. | 0 to 1,000,000 $ | 50 |
| Barber, per visit | Charged once when anything changes. | 0 to 1,000,000 $ | 100 |
| Paid from | Money accounts tried in order until one has enough, for example cash then bank. | up to 5 accounts | cash, bank |

**Clothing store blip** and **Barber shop blip**: the look of the map blips.

| Setting | What it changes | Values | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blip look (clothing) | Picture, colour and size of clothing store blips. | sprite 0-1000, colour 0-100, size 0.3-2 | sprite 366, colour 47, size 0.7 |
| Blip look (barber) | The same for barber shops. | sprite 0-1000, colour 0-100, size 0.3-2 | sprite 71, colour 0, size 0.7 |

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

None of its own. Stores open with ai-lib's interaction key (**E**), or with the target eye when the owner picked target interaction.

## Works with other scripts

- **AI Core reads and applies looks through its appearance layer**, so the character screen, sleeping bodies (ai-exitsleeping) and other scripts dress peds with whichever clothing script runs.
- **You can replace it.** AI Core also works with tgiann-clothing, illenium-appearance, qb-clothing, crm-appearance, rcore_clothing and bl_appearance. Any of them makes ai-appearance unneeded: AI Core tells you in the console to remove it. See [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility).

## Known limits

- No tattoos, outfits, wardrobe, clothing as items, per-item prices, ped models or store ownership.
- Job uniforms put on by other scripts (AI Core's `WearOutfit`) are never saved; the saved look comes back with `ReloadSkin` or the next login.
- Clothing that exists only in the GTA V Enhanced edition is left out of the catalog (it shows broken on FiveM Legacy).
