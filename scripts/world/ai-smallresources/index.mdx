ai-smallresources collects the small features most servers want: a seatbelt, cruise control, crouching, pointing, hands up, sitting on benches, carrying players, small items, GTA world tweaks, population density, teleports, elevators and Discord Rich Presence. Every feature has its own switch in the panel, so you turn off what another script already does.

## What it does in the game

**Driving**
- **Seatbelt** (**K**): a buckled player cannot leave the vehicle and survives faster crashes. Without the belt, a crash above the panel's speed throws the driver through the windscreen. HUDs show the belt state.
- **Cruise control** (**Y**): holds the current speed as the vehicle's top speed until pressed again.
- **No seat shuffle**, **no vehicle radio**, **no mouse steering** for land vehicles and boats, **no steering in the air** (a jump lands the way it took off).
- **Fake plate** item (`fakeplate`): fits a random plate on a vehicle you have the keys to (a 6 second bar facing the vehicle); using it again puts the real plate back. Garages keep the real plate.
- **Boat rope**: on foot next to a boat within 12 m of a mooring (Moorings tab), **Tie the boat to the dock** freezes it so it cannot drift or be driven off; **Untie the boat** frees it.

**Player**
- **Crouch** (**Left Ctrl**), **Point with finger** (**B**, everyone sees it), **Hands up** (**X**, also in a vehicle), **Fall over** (hold **M** on foot).
- **Sit on objects**: chairs, benches and stools from the Seats tab (364 models come ready) get a **Sit down** prompt; **X** stands up. With a target script running, the seats use the target eye instead of a prompt.
- **Carry players**: the radial menu's **Carry** item picks up the nearest player over the shoulder, or puts them down. A downed or cuffed player is picked up at once; anyone else is asked first (10 seconds to answer). After a refusal you wait 30 seconds before asking again.
- **Tackle** (**G** while sprinting): knocks down the player right in front.
- **Block corner peeking**, **sprint indoors**, **no automatic helmet**, **hats stay on**, **no idle camera**, **AFK kick** after the panel's minutes (admins are never kicked).

**Combat**
- No combat stance after a fight, no pistol whip, no blind peeking from cover, optional no one shot headshots, no bunny hopping, no GTA pickups, no weapons from vehicles.

**Items**
- `binoculars`: a zoom camera (mouse wheel zooms, **Backspace** puts them away).
- `firework1` to `firework4`: placed on the ground (3 second bar), they go off after 5 seconds; players within 400 m see them.
- `notepad`: writes a page that becomes a `note` item; using the note shows its text (the inventory tooltip shows it too).
- `newscam`, `newsmic`, `newsboom`: the news crew props, held in the hand and seen by everyone. With the camera, **H** switches to the news camera view.
- `diving_gear`: puts an air tank and mask on or takes them off; while worn the player breathes underwater until the tank is empty. `diving_fill` refills the tank. Air is kept per character and spent only underwater.

**World and population**
- No GTA wanted level or AI police, no GTA police and emergency vehicles or NPCs, areas without GTA-parked vehicles (hospitals, police stations, the prison, bases), no GTA planes, no police radio, quiet city sounds, hidden GTA HUD parts, no garbage trucks.
- Pedestrian, scenario people, traffic and parked car density in percent.

**Places**
- **Teleports**: a prompt at both ends (**Go: ...**) takes the player to the other end. Two come ready (Weazel News office, Coke processing).
- **Elevators**: a prompt on every floor (**...: choose a floor**) lists the others. One comes ready (Humane Labs).

**Discord Rich Presence**: your application's name, pictures, two text lines with live placeholders and up to two link buttons on every player's Discord profile.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-smallresources` after `ai-core`.

The items must exist in your inventory's item list: `binoculars`, `firework1`, `firework2`, `firework3`, `firework4`, `newscam`, `newsmic`, `newsboom`, `fakeplate`, `notepad`, `note`, `diving_gear`, `diving_fill` (ai-inventory has them).

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Small features**. Six tabs. Defaults are from `data/settings.json`.

### Tab: Features

Small gameplay features, each with its own switch.

#### Driving

Seatbelt, cruise control, seats, radio, plates and boats.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seatbelt | K buckles up in cars; a buckled player cannot leave the vehicle and survives faster crashes. | on / off | on |
| Thrown out without belt | A crash above this speed throws the driver through the windscreen. 0-500. | km/h | 20 |
| Thrown out with belt | The same speed for a buckled driver. 0-500. | km/h | 160 |
| Cruise control | Y holds the current speed as the vehicle's top speed until pressed again. | on / off | on |
| No seat shuffle | A passenger stays in their seat when the driver gets out. | on / off | on |
| No vehicle radio | Vehicle radios stay off and the radio wheel does not open. | on / off | off |
| Fake plate item | Fits a random plate on a vehicle the player has the keys to; using it on the vehicle again puts the real plate back. Garages keep the real plate. | on / off | on |
| Boat rope | On foot next to a boat near a mooring (Moorings tab), the player ties it so it cannot drift or be driven off, and unties it again. | on / off | on |
| No mouse steering | Cars, bikes and boats cannot be steered with the mouse. Planes and helicopters still can. | on / off | on |
| No steering in the air | A car or bike in the air cannot be tilted or rolled with the keys, so a jump lands the way it took off. | on / off | on |

| Fall over | While its key is held on foot the player falls over and lies limp. | on / off | on |
| Sit on objects | Chairs, benches and stools from the Seats tab get a Sit down prompt. | on / off | on |
| Carry players | From the radial menu a player carries the nearest player over the shoulder. Downed or cuffed players are picked up at once, anyone else is asked first. | on / off | on |
| Tackle | G while sprinting knocks down the player right in front. | on / off | on |
| Block corner peeking | A player cannot shoot at what only the third person camera sees round a corner: firing stops while the gun barrel is behind cover. | on / off | on |
| Sprint indoors | Players can sprint inside buildings too, not only outside. | on / off | on |
| No automatic helmet | Getting on a motorbike or into an aircraft does not put a helmet on by itself. | on / off | on |

#### Combat

How fights and weapons feel.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No combat stance | After a punch or a shot the player walks normally again at once instead of staying in GTA's hunched combat walk. | on |
| No pistol whip | With a gun in hand the melee keys do nothing, so close up the player shoots instead of hitting with the gun. | on |
| No blind peeking from cover | A player in cover cannot lean out to look without aiming. | on |
| No one shot headshots | A headshot on a player does the same damage as a body shot. | off |
| No bunny hopping | A second jump within 5 seconds makes the player trip and fall. | on |
| No GTA pickups | Weapons, ammo, armour, health and money that GTA drops (from dead NPCs, for example) cannot be picked up. Items come only from the inventory. | on |
| No weapons from vehicles | Getting into a police car or another armed vehicle gives no weapon. | on |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Firework items | Fireworks are placed on the ground and go off after 5 seconds. | on / off | on |
| Notepad item | Using the notepad writes a page that becomes a note item; using the note shows its text. | on / off | on |

| Putting on / taking off | Seconds the animation takes. 1-60. | s | 5 |
| Refilling | Seconds refilling the tank with a diving_fill item takes. 1-60. | s | 5 |

#### World

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |

| No GTA police and emergency vehicles | GTA spawns no police, ambulance, fire or army vehicles and no police, medic, guard or soldier NPCs anywhere. Vehicles and NPCs that scripts create are not affected. | on |

| Quiet city sounds | Mutes far away sirens, car alarms and the music GTA plays while flying. | on |

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pedestrians | Walking people, in percent of GTA's normal amount. 0 empties the streets. | % | 80 |
| Scenario people | People sitting, working or standing at fixed places, in percent. | % | 80 |
| Traffic | Moving AI vehicles, in percent. | % | 80 |
| Parked cars | Cars parked along the streets, in percent. | % | 80 |
| No garbage trucks | GTA's garbage trucks that stop in the middle of the road do not drive around. | on / off | on |

### Tab: Teleports

Doors that take players between two points. One record per teleport (**New teleport** / **Delete teleport**). A prompt at both ends takes the player to the other end.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Shown on the prompt (`Go: Weazel News office`). |
| First end | One end, with the heading the player faces on arrival. |
| Second end | The other end. |
| Jobs | Only members of these jobs can use it. Leave jobs and gangs empty for everyone. |
| Gangs | Members of these gangs can use it too. |
| Only on duty | Job members must be on duty in that job. |

### Tab: Elevators

Elevators with several floors: a prompt on every floor lists the others. One record per elevator (**New elevator** / **Delete elevator**).

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The elevator's name on the prompt. |
| Floors | At least two, up to 20, top to bottom or in any order you like. Each row has a **Floor name**, a **Floor point** (the player arrives there facing its heading) and a **Job** (only that job's members may go to that floor; empty for everyone). |

### Tab: Moorings

Dock spots boats can be tied to with the boat rope. One record per mooring (**New mooring** / **Delete mooring**). 16 docks come ready. A boat floating within 12 m of the spot can be tied.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The mooring's name. |
| Mooring spot | Stand at the edge of the dock where boats lie. |

### Tab: Seats

World objects players can sit on, and where on them they sit. One record per object model (**New seat object** / **Delete seat object**). Every object of this model in the world gets a Sit down prompt.

| Setting | What it changes | Default for new |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Object | The chair, bench or stool model. | |
| How to sit | The sitting pose: Chair, Armchair, Bench, Bar stool, Deck chair, Sun lounger, Club seat (watching), Low wall, Steps, Ledge (legs hanging), Tattoo chair, Lounging, Director's chair. | Chair |
| Place straight on the seat | For stools and booths the player cannot walk into; otherwise they walk to the seat. | off |
| Seats on the object | One row per place to sit (up to 12), measured from the object's centre: **Right**, **Forward**, **Up** (each -10 to 10 m) and **Facing**; heading 180 faces the object's front. The nearest free seat is taken. | |

### Tab: Discord

What players' Discord profiles show while they play on this server (Rich Presence).

**Discord application**: create an application at discord.com/developers (Applications > New Application). Its name is the title Discord shows above the lines, for example your server's name.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord Rich Presence | Shows your application's name, pictures, text lines and buttons on every player's Discord profile. Needs the application id. Off: players keep FiveM's own presence. | off |
| Application id | On discord.com/developers open your application, General Information, and copy the Application ID (17 to 20 digits). It is public, not a secret. | empty |

**Pictures**: upload the pictures in your application under Rich Presence > Art Assets (at least 512 x 512 px), then type their names here. A new picture can take a few minutes to show up in Discord.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Large picture | The name of an Art Asset of your application: the big square picture next to the text, usually your logo. Empty: no picture. |
| Large picture text | Shown when someone moves the mouse over the large picture. |
| Small picture | The name of an Art Asset of your application: the small round picture on the corner of the large one. Empty: no small picture. |
| Small picture text | Shown when someone moves the mouse over the small picture. |

**Text lines**: two lines under the application's name; Discord counts the time played next to them by itself. Both lines empty: FiveM's own text.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First line | The upper line, up to 128 characters. Updates by itself when a placeholder's value changes (the street at most every 15 seconds). | `{players}/{maxPlayers} players` |
| Second line | The lower line, up to 128 characters, with the same placeholders. | `Playing as {name}` |

Placeholders:

| Placeholder | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{players}` | Players online |
| `{maxPlayers}` | Server slots |
| `{name}` | Character name |
| `{id}` | Server id |
| `{job}` | Job |
| `{street}` | The street the player is on |

**Buttons**: up to two buttons under the presence that open a link, for example your Discord invite or website. Other people see and click them; Discord does not show them to the player himself.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Buttons | Two at most, shown in this order (**Add button**). **Button text**: up to 32 characters. **Link**: the page the button opens; it must start with `https://`. |

<Callout type="info">
Turning Rich Presence off gives FiveM's own presence back, but only if ai-smallresources set it. Another presence script running beside it is never overwritten.
</Callout>

## Other scripts

- **Emotes:** when tgiann-emotes, codem-emotemenu, rpemotes or 0r-animmenu runs, its hands up and pointing win and ai-smallresources' gesture keys stay off. AI Core's gestures layer answers other scripts from whichever runs.
- **Seat belt:** a HUD with its own seat belt (tgiann-lumihud, tgiann-hudv2, tgiann-modern-hud) or qbx_seatbelt is read first by AI Core's seat belt layer; turn the Seatbelt switch off when you use one of them.

## Known limits

- Hands up never starts over another script's animation (handcuffs, a surrender), and ends when that animation is replaced.
- Areas without parked vehicles empty every GTA-parked car in the box, not only emergency ones: keep the sizes small.
- Taking the `diving_gear` item away while it is worn does not take the gear off (as in Qbox). A restart or character switch does.
- Fireworks cannot be placed from a vehicle.
