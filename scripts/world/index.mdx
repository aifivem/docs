These scripts shape the world around the players and give admins their tools in the game.

| Script | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [ai-properties](/docs/scripts/world/ai-properties) | Houses and apartments with a private interior, a stash and keys. |
| [ai-doorlock](/docs/scripts/world/ai-doorlock) | Door locks picked in the world, with job and gang access. |
| [ai-smallresources](/docs/scripts/world/ai-smallresources) | Seatbelt, cruise control, crouch, sitting, carrying and many small features. |
| [ai-exitsleeping](/docs/scripts/world/ai-exitsleeping) | Logged-out players stay in the world as sleeping bodies. |
| [ai-pedscale](/docs/scripts/world/ai-pedscale) | Character height and width. |
| [ai-admin](/docs/scripts/world/ai-admin) | Noclip, teleports, revive, vehicles and a quick menu for admins. |
