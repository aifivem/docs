ai-exitsleeping keeps logged-out characters in the world. When a player leaves the server or goes back to the character screen, their character lies down as a sleeping body where they stood. Other players can carry it, put it into a vehicle and take it out again, and the owner wakes up wherever the body ended up.

## What it does in the game

- **Sleeping bodies.** The body has the character's look (through AI Core's appearance layer, so any supported clothing script works) and one of the panel's lying poses. Every player sees the same pose.
- **Carrying.** Walk up to a body and press the interact key at **Carry Sleeping Player**. **X** (or **Stop Carrying**) puts it down where you stand. You cannot carry while in a vehicle, downed or cuffed.
- **Vehicles.** While carrying, **Put in Vehicle** at a vehicle seats the body in a passenger seat. **Take Out Sleeping Player** at a vehicle with a body inside takes it out (you then carry it).
- **Waking up.** The owner's next spawn is where the body is now: on the ground, on the carrier's shoulder, or in the vehicle seat it was put in.
- **Expiry.** Bodies of players who logged out longer ago than the panel's days are removed.
- **Hidden areas and private worlds.** No body is left inside a hidden area (panel), and bodies put down there are hidden. With **Ignore private worlds** on, players inside a house or motel interior leave no body and see none.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-exitsleeping` after `ai-core`. The table `ai_exitsleeping_sleepers` is created on the first start.

If your spawn script places the player itself after ai-core's spawn, set **Wake up on** to **PlayerLoaded export (spawn script)** and call `exports['ai-exitsleeping']:PlayerLoaded()` from it (see the [developer page](/docs/scripts/world/ai-exitsleeping/developer)).

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Exit Sleeping**.

### Tab: General

How long sleeping bodies stay, how far they are drawn and what players can do with them.

**Sleeping bodies**: a player who logs out stays in the world as a sleeping body where they left, and wakes up where the body ended up.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep bodies for | Bodies of players who logged out longer ago than this are removed. 1-365. | days | 3 |
| Draw distance | Sleeping bodies appear when a player comes this close and disappear past it. 5-60, steps of 0.5. | m | 25 |
| Ignore private worlds | Players inside a private world (a house or motel room interior) leave no body when they log out and see no bodies while inside. | on / off | on |
| Wake up on | **Character spawn (ai-core)** or **PlayerLoaded export (spawn script)**. Keep the default unless a spawn script calls `exports['ai-exitsleeping']:PlayerLoaded()` after its own spawn. | | Character spawn (ai-core) |

**Carrying and vehicles**: players can carry a sleeping body, put it into a vehicle seat and take it out again.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interaction distance | How close a player must stand to carry a body or reach a vehicle. 1-10, steps of 0.5. | m | 2.5 |
| Server distance check | The server refuses a carry or vehicle action from further away than this (against cheats). 2-30, steps of 0.5. | m | 6 |
| Position check | How often the server updates where carried bodies and bodies in vehicles are, so the owner wakes up at the right place. 1-60. | s | 5 |
| Debug messages | Prints what the script does to the server and client consoles. | on / off | off |

### Tab: Animations

The lying poses of sleeping bodies and the carry animation. Each animation is an **Animation dictionary**, an **Animation name** and **Animation flags** (GTA animation flags, 0-65535: 1 loops, 49 loops on the upper body while walking, 33 loops while attached).

| Card | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lying poses | Every body picks one of these at random; every player sees the same pose. Up to 20. | `amb@world_human_bum_slumped@male@laying_on_left_side@idle_a` / `idle_b`, flags 1 |
| Carrying player | The animation of the player who carries a body. | `missfinale_c2mcs_1` / `fin_c2_mcs_1_camman`, flags 49 |
| Carried body | The animation of the body on the carrier's shoulder. | `nm` / `firemans_carry`, flags 33 |

**Carry position**: where the carried body sits on the carrier.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bone index | Bone index of the carrier the body is attached to (0 = the ped's root). | | 0 |
| Offset right | -5 to 5, steps of 0.01. | m | 0.2 |
| Offset forward | -5 to 5. | m | 0.15 |
| Offset up | -5 to 5. | m | 0.63 |
| Rotation X | -360 to 360, steps of 0.5. | ° | 0.5 |
| Rotation Y | -360 to 360. | ° | 0.5 |
| Rotation Z | -360 to 360. | ° | 5 |

### Tab: Hidden areas

Places where no sleeping body is shown, such as interiors or safe zones. One record per area (**New area** / **Delete area**). Deleting an area makes the sleeping bodies inside visible again. None come ready.

**Area**: players who log out inside leave no body; bodies put down inside are hidden (the owner still wakes up there).

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The area's name. | | |
| Shape | **Ball**, **Cylinder** or **Polygon**: a ball or a cylinder around a centre point, or a polygon from corner points. | | Ball |
| Centre | The middle of the area (ball and cylinder). | point | |
| Radius | Metres from the centre to the edge (ball and cylinder). 1-2000. | m | 25 |
| Height | Metres up and down the area reaches (cylinder: from the centre; polygon: past its lowest and highest corner). 0.5-1000. | m | 10 |

**Polygon corners** (polygon only): add a corner at each turn of the border, in order (free camera or your position); a polygon needs at least three, up to 64. The shape is drawn in the world while this field is on screen.

## Known limits

- Bodies exist only in the shared world: inside an instanced interior a carried body is put down at its last outside position.
- After a server restart every body lies on the ground at its last saved position (carried and seated states are not kept).
- A player who wakes within a few seconds of their carrier or vehicle disappearing may spawn at the last saved spot (the Position check interval).
