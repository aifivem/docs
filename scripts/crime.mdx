## ai-robberies

Store registers and safes, Fleeca banks (vault door and lockers), Vangelico vitrines, house robberies in private interiors, and the armored truck job. Minimum police on duty, cooldowns, rewards and alerts are set in the panel; every step is checked on the server.

## ai-drugs

Street sales to strangers (from the radial menu, a live ambient ped must stand next to you) and weed plants that grow over time, stored in the database and shown only near them.

## ai-pawnshop

Pawnshops buying loot at panel prices, scrapyards taking unregistered (stolen) vehicles apart for materials.

## ai-lapraces

Tracks drawn in the panel, a lobby at the start line, checkpoints checked by the server, best times stored per track.
