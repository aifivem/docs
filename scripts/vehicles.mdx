## ai-vehicles

Owned vehicles of characters, jobs and gangs: stored state and tuning kept, one take-out at a time per vehicle. Answers the **qbx_vehicles** API, so Qbox scripts see owned vehicles.

## ai-vehiclekeys

Keys for owned vehicles (the owner and members of the owning job or gang), keys given by scripts (rentals, job vehicles, robberies), locking with a key fob, lockpicking and hotwiring (with a lockpick or skill check minigame, chosen in the panel). Answers the **qbx_vehiclekeys** API. Other key systems are supported through AI Core's key layer.

## ai-fuel

Fuel use per vehicle class, gas stations at every map pump: take the nozzle, walk to the car, fill up and pay. Electric vehicles charge instead. The fuel level is shared through the vehicle, so any script reads it.

## ai-garages

Public garages, job fleet garages and gang garages (shown only to members; a gang garage also keeps its members' own cars), impound lots with a fee. Park and take out with a list of your vehicles; the garage blips and NPCs are set in the panel.

## ai-vehicleshop

Vehicle shops with a catalog and a live preview, categories and prices in the panel. A bought vehicle goes to the buyer's garage with new keys.

## ai-vehiclesales

Used car lots: a player drives an owned vehicle onto the lot, sets a price and it is parked there for everyone to see. Others browse the listings, take a timed test drive and buy; the seller is paid at once, or at their next login when offline (minus the lot's cut). The vehicle keeps its plate, look and trunk. A vehicle can also be sold straight to the dealer for a share of its catalog price. Lots, spots, prices, the dealer's share, where the lot's cut goes (a job or gang account) and test drive length are set in the panel.

## ai-rental

Rental points: a vehicle list with fee and deposit, the renter in the driver seat with keys and a full tank, the deposit back on return (scaled by the vehicle's condition), the rental time in the panel.

## ai-mechanic

Mechanic shops (public or job shops with a stash): repair, full tuning with a live preview and prices per part, car washes, a repair kit item.

## ai-sirens

Emergency vehicle lights and siren tones, an airhorn, siren control keys for the driver, and ONX lightbars for unmarked vehicles.
