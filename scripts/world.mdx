## ai-properties

Houses and apartments: buy at the door, a private interior per property, a stash, keys for other characters, sell back, police entry.

## ai-doorlock

Door locks set in the panel by picking the door in the world: job and gang access, the lock state the same for everyone. The prompt floats on the door itself.

## ai-smallresources

Many small features, each switchable in the panel: seatbelt (with the HUD warning), cruise control, crouch, pointing, hands up, sitting on chairs and benches (with the target eye when a target script runs, so no prompt follows you past every bench), carrying players, tackle, anti ghost-peek, usable items (binoculars, fireworks, notepad...), fake plates, dive gear with an air tank, boat ropes, teleports, elevators, population density and world tweaks, AFK kick.

## ai-exitsleeping

A player who logs out stays in the world as a sleeping body where they left. Others can carry it, put it into a vehicle and take it out again; the owner wakes up where the body ended up. Hidden areas and private worlds are respected.

## ai-pedscale

Character height and width set by admins (a panel tab, `/setheight`, `/setwidth`, `/resetscale`), within limits from the panel, seen by every player.

## ai-admin

Admin tools: noclip, teleport to waypoint, to a player or bring them, heal and revive, spawn and delete vehicles, coordinates, announcements, player warnings, weather and time, item commands, and a quick menu (F9). The QB / ESX commands admins know work too: `setjob`, `setgang`, `givemoney`, `setmoney`, `dvall`, `addpermission`, `closeserver`, `togglepvp` and more.
