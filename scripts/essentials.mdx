## ai-core

The framework itself.

- **Accounts and characters.** Players are identified by their FiveM account. Several characters per account, set in the panel; deleting can be turned off.
- **Character screen.** The characters stand in a real scene of the city (each in their own look); the camera glides to the picked one. Creating a character asks for name, birth date, sex and nationality.
- **Choosing where to appear.** Optional: after picking a character, a list of places (the last location and the places you set) appears over the scene.
- **Money.** Cash, bank, black money and crypto by default, more accounts in the panel. Every change is written with a ledger row; job and gang accounts too.
- **Jobs and gangs.** Several jobs and gangs per character (limits in the panel), one job on duty at a time, salary only from the on-duty job, optional prime time hours with lower pay outside them.
- **Admin panel** for everything (see [Admin panel](/docs/admin-panel)), logs to the console, Discord, Fivemanage or FiveM Stats, update checks of every script, script recommendations.
- **Shared UI** every script uses: notifications, the progress bar (one cancel key for all), menus, input dialogs, key hints on the HUD, the item picker for admins.
- **Bridge.** ESX, QBCore and Qbox scripts run on AI Core unchanged (see [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility)).

## ai-mysql

The database engine. Every `ai-*` script runs its own small connection pool; ai-mysql also stands in for **oxmysql**, **mysql-async** and **ghmattimysql**, so third-party scripts keep working. Do not install oxmysql next to it. Each script creates and updates its own tables at start.

## ai-lib

The shared player UI every script uses: notification cards, the progress bar and its cancel key, the prompts on the right and the interaction key, menus, input dialogs, and the lockpick and skill check minigames. It runs without ai-core, so other resources can use it on their own. It also carries the AI Core helpers for resources that do not bundle them: Lua resources add `shared_script '@ai-lib/init.lua'` and use `aiLib.notify`, `aiLib.Points`, `aiLib.addCommand`, `aiLib.progress` and more; JavaScript resources load `@ai-lib/dist/client.js` or `server.js`. See [the lib](/docs/developers/lib).

## ai-loading

The loading screen: your server name, subtitle and tagline from the General settings, a background picture, the loading progress and tips you write in the panel. It closes itself when the character screen is ready.

## ai-appearance

Character creator for new characters (in a private room), clothing shops and barbers at places you set, outfits, prices per shop. Character screen portraits come from it.

## ai-inventory

A grid inventory with weight and slots, a separate hotbar, drops on the ground, stashes, vehicle trunks and gloveboxes (an owned vehicle's trunk stays with the vehicle, whatever plate it wears), shops (with NPCs and job locks), crafting benches with recipes, weapons as items, rarities with colours, and the character drawn next to the inventory. It answers the **ox_inventory** and **qb-inventory** APIs.

## ai-hud

Health, armour, hunger, thirst and stress, money, the job, the street name, a speedometer with fuel and seatbelt warning, voice range and radio, and the key hints of the moment. Players set its look in a settings menu (radial menu, **HUD**). Needs are stored on the character and read by QB scripts too.

## ai-chat

Chat with channels (everyone, nearby, job), command suggestions for only the commands a player may use, colour codes, and the FiveM `chat` API for other scripts.

## ai-voice

Proximity voice with ranges, radio channels (job channels locked to the job), phone calls, on both FiveM Legacy (Mumble) and FiveM for GTA V Enhanced. Stands in for **pma-voice**.

## ai-radialmenu

The radial menu (F1): built-in actions, vehicle actions and the items other scripts add (police menu, sentence time, HUD settings, key list...).

## ai-target

Markers on what you can interact with (the target style). Scripts choose nothing: the owner picks **prompt** or **target** in the panel and every script's places follow. Answers the **ox_target** API.

## ai-scoreboard

Hold **Home**: players online, on-duty counts per job and, if you allow it, names and ids.

## ai-weathersync

Time and weather the same for everyone: clock speed, start hour, frozen time, weather cycle and blackout in the panel, weather regions. Inside interiors scripts pause the sync through AI Core, whatever weather script you use.
