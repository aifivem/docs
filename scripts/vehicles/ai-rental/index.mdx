ai-rental lets players rent a vehicle at a counter for a fee and a deposit. The deposit comes back when the vehicle is returned in time, less if it is damaged.

## What it does in the game

- Walk to a rental counter (within 2 m) and press the interact key at **Rent a vehicle at ...**. A menu lists the vehicles with their fee, deposit and rental time.
- Pick one: the fee and the deposit are taken together from the panel's account. The vehicle appears at the point's spawn spot with a full tank, a plate starting with the panel's plate start (for example `RNT12345`), the keys, and you in the driver seat.
- Bring it back near the counter (within the return radius) and press the interact key at **Give the rental back**. The deposit comes back scaled by the vehicle's condition (engine and body health): a perfect car returns the whole deposit, a wreck returns nothing.
- When the rental time runs out, the vehicle disappears and the deposit is kept. A rental time of 0 runs until the vehicle is given back.
- One rental per player at a time. Leaving the server ends the rental: the vehicle is deleted and the deposit kept.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-rental` after `ai-core`. No database table. One rental point comes ready: LSIA Car Rental at the airport, renting a Dinka Blista, a Declasse Asea, a Bravado Bison and a Pegassi Faggio for 60 minutes.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Vehicle rental**.

### Tab: Rental points

Where players rent vehicles, which vehicles of the list they can rent there and for how long. One record per point (**New rental point** / **Delete rental point**). Deleting a point removes it, its blip and its vehicle list; vehicles rented there now stay until they are given back or their time ends.

**Rental point** card: the counter players walk to and where the rented vehicle appears.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Shown on the prompt and the menu. 1-48 characters. | | |
| Counter | Players rent here (2 m) and give vehicles back around it (return radius in Settings). | point | |
| Vehicle spawn | Where and facing which way the rented vehicle appears; the renter sits in it at once. | point + heading | |
| Map blip | Shows this point on the map. | on / off | on |
| Blip look | Icon, color and size of this point's blip. | | icon 225, color 2, size 0.7 |
| Rental time | When it runs out the vehicle disappears and the deposit is kept. 0 = until given back. 0-1440. | min | 60 |

**Vehicles** card: vehicles of the vehicle list this point rents out; fee and deposit are set in the Vehicles tab.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicles | Up to 30 rows (**Add vehicle**), each one picked from the Vehicles tab list. |

### Tab: Vehicles

Every vehicle that can be rented, with its name, fee and deposit; rental points pick from this list. One record per vehicle (**New vehicle** / **Delete vehicle**). Deleting a vehicle removes it from every rental point that offered it; vehicles rented now stay until they are given back or their time ends.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model | The game's model name of the vehicle (addon vehicles too). Up to 32 characters. | | blista |
| Name | The name in the rental menu. 1-48 characters. | | |
| Fee | Paid when renting, never given back. 0-1,000,000. | $ | 150 |
| Deposit | Paid when renting, given back when the vehicle is returned in time. 0-1,000,000. | $ | 300 |

### Tab: Settings

Money and plates of every rental. Applies to every rental point.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paid from | The ai-core account the fee and deposit are taken from and the deposit goes back to. | | bank |
| Plate start | Up to 4 letters in front of the plate number, so rented vehicles are easy to spot. The plate is 8 characters in total. | | RNT |
| Return radius | How close to the counter a rented vehicle must be to give it back. 3-50. | m | 15 |

## Commands and keybinds

None. The prompts use the interact key (**E** by default).

## Known limits

- Rentals live only in memory: a resource restart deletes every rented vehicle and keeps the deposits.
- A vehicle parked on the spawn spot blocks renting ("Something is parked on the spawn spot").
- Only one account pays: fee and deposit together must fit in it.
