ai-fuel makes vehicles use fuel while they drive and lets players fill up at gas stations. The fuel level lives on the vehicle itself (the `fuel` state bag, 0-100), so the HUD, garages and any other script read the same number.

## What it does in the game

- **Fuel use.** The game burns fuel by engine revs: high revs burn more. A full tank lasts about 25 minutes of driving at the default burn rate. Bicycles, vehicles without a tank and the panel's electric models burn nothing.
- **Refuelling.** At a gas station, walk to a pump and take the nozzle (prompt **Take the nozzle**). Walk to your vehicle (the hose reaches 8 m from the pump) and plug it in (prompt **Refuel the vehicle**). A progress bar runs; when it ends, the tank is full and the price is taken from the first account that can pay it. Hang the nozzle back up with **Hang up the nozzle**. Walking past the hose's end or getting into a vehicle puts the nozzle back on its own.
- **Everyone sees it.** The nozzle in a player's hand and the hose from the pump are drawn for every player near.
- **Owned vehicles.** The fuel of an owned vehicle is saved in [ai-vehicles](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicles) whenever the whole number changes, so it is the same after parking or a restart.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-fuel` after `ai-core` (and after `ai-vehicles` if you use it). 27 gas stations around the map come ready in `data/stations.json`.

Do not run a second fuel script next to it: two scripts would burn the same tank.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Fuel**.

### Tab: Gas stations

Where players refuel and what it costs. One record per station (**New station** / **Delete station**). Only pumps inside a station's area sell fuel.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The station's name. 1-48 characters. | | |
| Centre | Stand in the middle of the pumps and use your position. | point | |
| Area | How far from the centre a pump counts as part of this station. 2-40. | m | 12 |
| Price | Dollars per percent of a tank (a full fill from empty costs 100 times this). 0-1000, steps of 0.5. | $ | 3 |
| Map blip | Shows this station on the map, with the look from **Station blip** below. | on / off | on |

### Tab: Settings

How fast vehicles use fuel and how refuelling is paid.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burn rate | 1 is normal (a full tank lasts about 25 minutes of driving), 2 burns twice as fast, 0 turns fuel off. 0-10, steps of 0.1. | x | 1 |
| Refuel time | Seconds per percent filled. A fill from empty takes 100 times this; the bar runs at least 1 second. 0-5, steps of 0.05. | s | 0.15 |
| Paid from | Money accounts tried in order. The whole price comes from one account. | | cash, bank |
| Electric models | Vehicle models (spawn names) that burn no fuel; add your add-on electric cars here. | | voltic, voltic2, raiden, cyclone, tezeract, neon, surge, dilettante, dilettante2, imorgon, khamelion, iwagen, omnisegt, virtue, caddy, caddy2, caddy3, airtug, rcbandito |
| Station blip | How gas stations look on the map (icon, color, size); each station switches its blip on or off. | | icon 361, color 0, size 0.7 |

## Commands and keybinds

None. The pump prompts use the interact key (**E** by default).

## Logs

Each paid refuel goes to the `refuels` log channel (station, percent filled, price).

## Other fuel scripts

AI Core's fuel layer also works with ox_fuel, Renewed-Fuel, codem-xfuelv2, LegacyFuel, cdn-fuel, ps-fuel, lc_fuel, okokGasStation and x-fuel. Run one of them instead of ai-fuel (never both). Pick it in the admin panel: **Server** > **General** > **Fuel script** (**Automatic** takes the first one running). ai-* scripts read and set fuel through the layer, so they keep working.

## Known limits

- Electric models simply burn nothing. There are no charging stations.
- Refuelling always fills the tank to 100 %. There is no partial fill.
- The nozzle sits on the fuel cap bone of the vehicle. On models without one (some vans and trucks), it uses a rough spot near a rear wheel or the engine and may look out of place.
- The nozzle does not follow a vehicle that moves while it is plugged in.
