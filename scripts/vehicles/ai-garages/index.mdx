ai-garages is where players park their vehicles and take them out again. It keeps no vehicles of its own: every vehicle lives in [ai-vehicles](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicles), ai-garages only shows the list and moves vehicles in and out.

## What it does in the game

- **Parking.** Drive into a garage's area and press the interact key at the **Park in ...** prompt. Everyone else has to get out first. The vehicle is saved with its look, fuel and damage. Vehicles are stored per garage: a car parked at Motel Parking waits there, not at every garage.
- **Taking out.** On foot inside the area, press the interact key at **Open ...**. A list shows your vehicles with fuel, engine and body condition and the plate. **Take out** brings the vehicle to the garage's exit point, and you get the keys.
- **Job and gang garages.** A garage with jobs or gangs set shows only to their members and lists that job's or gang's fleet. Fleet vehicles without a garage show in every garage of their job or gang. A gang garage also keeps its members' own cars parked there.
- **Impound lots.** A vehicle left out when the server restarts, or deleted in the world, waits at the impound. An impound lot lists those vehicles; taking one out costs the impound fee (**Pay $...**). If the vehicle cannot be brought out, the fee is paid back.
- **Garage types.** Car, boat and aircraft garages each list only their own kind.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-garages` after `ai-core` and `ai-vehicles`. 37 garages come ready in `data/garages.json`: 16 public car parks, the Depot Lot impound, a police garage, the Ballas, Families, Lost MC and Cartel gang garages, five aircraft hangars, three boathouses, the air, sea and big rig depots, and five big rig parkings.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Garages**.

### Tab: Garages

Where players park and take out their vehicles; job and gang garages hold their fleet, impound lots hold towed vehicles. One record per garage (**New garage** / **Delete garage**). Deleting a garage keeps the vehicles parked there stored; admins can move them.

**Garage** card: the area where players park, and where vehicles come out.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for new |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Shown on the prompt and the list. 1-48 characters. | | |
| Vehicles | **Cars**, **Boats** or **Aircraft**: which vehicles park here, and which blip it uses. | | Cars |
| Area centre | Stand in the middle of the parking area and use your position. | point | |
| Area | How far from the centre players can park or open the garage. 2-50. | m | 12 |
| Exit point | Where taken out vehicles appear, facing your direction. | point + heading | |
| Jobs | Only members of these jobs can use it. Leave jobs and gangs empty for everyone. | | empty |
| Gangs | Members of these gangs can use it too. | | empty |
| Only on duty | Job members must be on duty in that job. | on / off | off |
| Impound lot | Lists towed and lost vehicles for a fee instead of parked ones. | on / off | off |
| Map blip | Shows this garage on the map, with the look of its kind from the Settings tab. | on / off | on |

**NPC** card: a person standing at this place. Leave the model empty for none.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Model | The ped model; empty means no NPC. Players see it within 50 m. |
| What it does | An idle animation the NPC plays on the spot (stands still, waits impatiently, holds a clipboard, looks at a phone, drinks coffee, smokes, stands guard, police idle, hangs out). |
| Where it stands | The NPC's floor spot and the way it faces; without one it stands on the place's point. |

### Tab: Settings

What getting a vehicle back from the impound costs, and how garages look on the map.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fee | Paid when taking a vehicle out of the impound. 0-1,000,000. | $ | 500 |
| Paid from | Money accounts tried in order. | | cash, bank |
| Cars / Boats / Aircraft / Impound lot (Map blips) | The blip of each garage kind (icon, color, size); a garage shows it when its Map blip switch is on. | | Cars 357, Boats 356, Aircraft 360, Impound 68; color 3, size 0.6 |

## Commands and keybinds

None. The prompts use the interact key (**E** by default).

## Other garage scripts

AI Core's garage layer also works with tgiann-realparking, tgiann-garage, jg-advancedgarages and cd_garage. Pick one in the admin panel: **Server** > **General** > **Garage script** (**Automatic** takes the first one running) and stop ai-garages. Police impounds and other ai-* scripts go through the layer, so they keep working (each script answers only what it can do).

## Known limits

- The garage list opens only inside the garage's area: other scripts cannot open it from afar.
- Parking refuses a vehicle with other players inside, and a vehicle that is not owned (ai-vehicles).
- Parking keeps the stored tuning: only a tuning shop ([ai-mechanic](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-mechanic)) changes mods.
