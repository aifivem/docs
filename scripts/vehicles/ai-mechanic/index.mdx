ai-mechanic repairs and tunes vehicles. Shops can be open to everyone or run by a job (a mechanic job with its own stash, whose staff bill customers). It also runs car washes and the repair kit item.

## What it does in the game

- **Opening a shop.** Drive into a shop's area and, from the driver seat, press the interact key at **Open ...**. A menu opens with **Repair**, **Performance**, **Body parts**, **Wheels**, **Paint** and **Lights, tint and plate** (only what the shop offers).
- **Tuning with a preview.** Moving through a list shows each part on the car before you pay. Each purchase is one part: the price shows next to it, **Installed** marks what the car has. Closing a list puts back the last bought look.
- **Saved for good.** On an owned vehicle, bought tuning is saved in [ai-vehicles](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicles): ai-mechanic is the only script that changes a car's stored tuning.
- **Repair.** Fixes engine and body. The price follows the damage (price per damage point times up to 2000 points).
- **Who pays.** At a public shop the driver pays from the panel's accounts. At a job shop, only on-duty members of that job can work, and the job's `bank` account pays, except for a car the member's own character owns, which the member pays.
- **Job shop stash.** A job shop can have a shared stash (**Open the shop stash**) for its members.
- **Customer bills.** On-duty members of a job that runs a job shop see **Bill customer** in the radial menu. It bills the nearest player with an amount and a reason; the job's account gets the money when the customer pays the bill.
- **Car wash.** Drive into a car wash and press the interact key at **Wash the vehicle ($...)**. A progress bar runs in the car; the price is taken and the dirt removed at the end.
- **Repair kit.** Use the repair kit item on foot next to a vehicle (within 4 m). After a progress bar, the engine comes back up to the panel's value ("The engine runs again, get it to a mechanic"). The kit is used up only if you are still within 5 m.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-mechanic` after `ai-core` (and after `ai-vehicles` so tuning is saved). Add a `repairkit` item to your inventory if it has none (or set another item in the panel).

Default places: five shops (LS Customs Burton, a job shop of the `mechanic` job with price multiplier 0.8; LS Customs Harmony and Airport; Benny's Motorworks with multiplier 1.2; Beeker's Garage in Paleto with repairs only) and five car washes (Strawberry, Davis, Paleto, Sandy Shores, Little Seoul).

## Commands and keybinds

None. The prompts use the interact key (**E** by default). Customer bills are in the radial menu (**F3** by default).

## Logs

Log channels: `tuning` and `repairs` (off by default; each purchase and repair with its payer and vehicle id) and `bills`.

## Known limits

- Tuning, repair and wash need the driver seat.
- A tuning change on a vehicle that is not owned (a job car that is not in ai-vehicles, a rental) is shown but not saved: it is lost when the vehicle is deleted.
- Job shops always charge the job's `bank` shared account.
