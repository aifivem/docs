The vehicle scripts work together through AI Core: [ai-vehicles](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicles) stores every owned vehicle, and the other scripts buy, park, lock, fuel, repair and rent them. Keys, fuel and garages can each be replaced by a supported third-party script; the others keep working, because they ask AI Core instead of each other.

| Script | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [ai-vehicles](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicles) | Owned vehicles of characters, jobs and gangs: the storage the other vehicle scripts build on. Stands in for qbx_vehicles. |
| [ai-vehiclekeys](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehiclekeys) | Keys, locking (U), engine (M), lockpicking and hotwiring. Stands in for qbx_vehiclekeys. |
| [ai-fuel](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-fuel) | Fuel use and gas stations with a nozzle and hose; electric models burn nothing. |
| [ai-garages](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-garages) | Public, job and gang garages and impound lots with a fee. |
| [ai-vehicleshop](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicleshop) | Vehicle shops with a catalog, a showroom preview and colors. |
| [ai-vehiclesales](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehiclesales) | Used car lots: players list their vehicles, others test drive and buy; dealer buy-back. |
| [ai-rental](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-rental) | Rental points with a fee and a deposit. |
| [ai-mechanic](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-mechanic) | Repair, tuning with a live preview, job shops, car washes, repair kit. |
| [ai-sirens](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-sirens) | Emergency lights, siren tones, airhorn and ONX lightbars. |

## How they fit together

<Steps>
<Step>
A player buys a car at **ai-vehicleshop**. It is created in **ai-vehicles**, owned by their character, and comes out with the keys.
</Step>
<Step>
**ai-vehiclekeys** knows the owner from ai-vehicles: only the owner (or the members of an owning job or gang) can lock and start it.
</Step>
<Step>
**ai-fuel** burns the tank while driving; the level is saved with the vehicle.
</Step>
<Step>
**ai-garages** parks it with its look, fuel and damage. A vehicle lost in the world waits at the impound.
</Step>
<Step>
**ai-mechanic** repairs and tunes it. It is the only script that changes the car's stored tuning.
</Step>
<Step>
**ai-vehiclesales** lets the player sell it to another player, with the same plate, look and trunk.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Start order

In `server.cfg`, start `ai-vehicles` right after `ai-core`, then the other vehicle scripts. The AI Core recipe does this for you.
