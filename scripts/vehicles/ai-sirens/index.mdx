ai-sirens gives the driver of an emergency vehicle control over its lights and siren. Every player near sees the lights and hears the same tone.

## What it does in the game

Only emergency class vehicles (GTA class 18) listed in the panel get siren control. Sitting in the driver seat of one:

- **Lights.** **Q** turns the emergency lights on or off. Turning the lights off also stops the siren.
- **Siren.** **Left Alt** starts the siren on tone 1 (the lights must be on) or stops it.
- **Tone.** **Y** switches to the next tone (1, 2, 3, back to 1) while the siren runs. With the siren off, holding it plays the manual siren.
- **Airhorn.** Hold **E** for the airhorn.
- **Firetrucks** keep their own wail on tone 1, and their airhorn and tone 3 are fire truck sounds. **Ambulances** (and other listed models) play the powercall on tone 1.
- **Leaving the car.** With the panel's switch on, the siren sound stops when the driver gets out; the lights stay on.
- **ONX lightbars.** An unmarked car with the ONX lightbar pack gets a lightbar picked by a tuning slot (slot 49 by default). Each player's game draws its own copy, and it flashes with the vehicle's lights. Vehicles with an ONX lightbar use the ONX tones.
- GTA's far away police sirens that play on their own can be silenced.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-sirens` after `ai-core`.

For lightbars, stream the ONX lightbar models (`emergency-b-lightbars`) and set the vehicle's tuning slot to a value from the panel's list (at a tuning shop or with your own script).

<Callout type="warn">
Add-on police, fire and ambulance cars must be added to **Emergency vehicle models**, or the server refuses their siren changes.
</Callout>

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Sirens**.

### Tab: Sirens

How emergency vehicles sound: tones, airhorn, firetrucks and ambulances.

**General**: emergency vehicles (class 18) get the siren keys: lights, siren, tone, airhorn; every player near hears them.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No distant sirens | Silences GTA's far away police sirens that play on their own. | on |
| Siren stops when the driver gets out | On: leaving the driver seat turns the siren sound off (the lights stay). | on |
| Emergency vehicle models | Only these models get sirens, airhorn and lightbars; add your add-on police, fire and ambulance cars here. Empty: every emergency class vehicle. Up to 200. | ambulance, fbi, fbi2, firetruk, lguard, pbus, police, police2, police3, police4, policeb, policeold1, policeold2, policet, pranger, riot, riot2, sheriff, sheriff2 |
| Firetruck models | These keep their own wail on tone 1, their airhorn and tone 3 are the fire truck sounds. | firetruk |
| Ambulance tone models | Tone 1 of these models plays the ambulance powercall. | ambulance, firetruk, lguard |

**Tones**: the game sounds of the three siren tones for normal emergency vehicles.

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Tone 1 | `VEHICLES_HORNS_SIREN_1` |
| Tone 2 | `VEHICLES_HORNS_SIREN_2` |
| Tone 3 | `VEHICLES_HORNS_POLICE_WARNING` |

### Tab: Lightbars

ONX lightbars attached from a tuning slot, and the ONX siren tones.

**ONX lightbars**: needs the ONX lightbar models streamed (emergency-b-lightbars); each player's game draws its own copy, nothing spawns on the network.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attach lightbars | Off: no lightbars are attached. | on |
| Tuning slot | The vehicle mod slot that picks the lightbar (49 in the ONX pack). 0-49. | 49 |
| Models with ONX tones | These always use the ONX tones; vehicles with an ONX lightbar use them anyway. | empty |
| Lightbar per slot value | Which lightbar model each value of the tuning slot attaches (**Add a lightbar**: **Slot value** 0-100, **Lightbar model**). Up to 40 rows. | 4-13: `onx_pol_lbar_*` police bars, 14-16: `onx_ems_lbar_*`, 17-19: `onx_amb_lbar_*` |

**ONX tones**: the siren tones of vehicles with an ONX lightbar or listed above.

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Tone 1 | `RESIDENT_VEHICLES_SIREN_WAIL_02` |
| Tone 2 | `RESIDENT_VEHICLES_SIREN_QUICK_02` |
| Tone 3 | `VEHICLES_HORNS_POLICE_WARNING` |

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

Active only in the driver seat of an emergency vehicle. Players change them in GTA's key settings (FiveM section); the owner changes the defaults in the admin panel (**Keybinds**).

| Default key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Q | Siren: emergency lights on / off |
| Left Alt | Siren: main siren on / off |
| Y | Siren: next tone (hold for the manual siren while it is off) |
| E | Siren: airhorn (hold) |

While driving an emergency vehicle, the game's horn, radio and cinematic camera keys are taken over by these.

## Known limits

- The server cannot read a vehicle's class, so it trusts the **Emergency vehicle models** list. An empty list lets every model change siren state.
- The click sound from outside sound resources and pursuit mode tone sync of the original tgiann-siren-control are not included; a game sound plays instead of the click.
