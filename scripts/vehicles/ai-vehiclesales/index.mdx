ai-vehiclesales runs used car lots. A player drives an owned vehicle onto the lot, sets a price, and the car is parked there for everyone to see. Other players browse, take a timed test drive and buy. The seller is paid even when offline.

## What it does in the game

- **Selling.** Drive your own vehicle onto the lot's sell spot, alone, and press the interact key at **Put your vehicle up for sale**. Set a price (between the panel's lowest and highest price) and an optional description, and confirm. Your keys for that plate are taken. The vehicle keeps its plate, look and trunk: it is the same vehicle, held by the lot.
- **Dealer buy-back.** If the vehicle is in [ai-vehicleshop](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-vehicleshop)'s catalog, the sell spot first offers a menu: **List it for sale**, or **Sell to the dealer for $...** (a share of the catalog price, paid at once; the vehicle is deleted for good).
- **The lot.** Listed vehicles stand on the lot's parking spots, first listed first parked. They are locked and cannot be driven, pushed or damaged. Listings beyond the number of spots are still for sale on the counter's list.
- **Browsing.** At the counter, **Browse ...** opens a showroom screen: every listing with its price, seller, plate, how long ago it was listed and the seller's description. Search by name or plate, sort by newest, cheapest or priciest, or show only **My listings**.
- **Test drive.** A temporary copy of the vehicle appears at the delivery spot with you in the driver seat. A timer counts down; getting out ends it early. At the end the copy is deleted and you are back at the counter. One character waits a cooldown between drives.
- **Buying.** **Buy** takes the price from the first account that can pay. The vehicle becomes yours and waits at the delivery spot. If it cannot be brought out, it goes to the impound (pick it up at an [ai-garages](/docs/scripts/vehicles/ai-garages) impound lot).
- **Getting paid.** The seller receives the price minus the lot's cut, right away when online, or at their next login. If a phone script runs, the seller also gets a mail.
- **Taking back.** On the screen, **Take back** returns your own listing to you at the delivery spot.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-vehiclesales` after `ai-core` and `ai-vehicles` (and `ai-vehicleshop` for the dealer buy-back). The tables `ai_vehiclesales_listings` and `ai_vehiclesales_payouts` are created on the first start. One lot comes ready: Larry's Vehicle Sales in Sandy Shores, with 25 parking spots.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Used car lots**.

### Tab: Lots

Where players sell their vehicles to each other. One record per lot (**New lot** / **Delete lot**). Deleting a lot keeps its listings stored; they show again when a lot with the same key exists.

**Lot** card: the counter players browse from, where they drive in to sell and how far the parked cars show.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lot name | Shown on the prompt and the screen. 1-48 characters. | | |
| Counter | Stand where players open the list of vehicles for sale and use your position. | point | |
| Counter reach | How far from the counter the prompt shows. 1-10. | m | 2 |
| Lot size | Metres around the counter. Parked cars show to players inside, and buyers must be inside to buy or test drive. 20-300. | m | 80 |
| Sell spot | Where players drive in with the vehicle they want to sell. | point | |
| Map blip | Shows this lot on the map with the look from the Settings tab. | on / off | on |

**Parking spots** card: where listed vehicles stand on the lot, first listed first parked. More listings than spots are still for sale on the counter's list.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Spots | A list of positions with heading (**Add spot**), up to 100. |

**Preview and delivery** card: where the vehicle a player looks at stands, where the camera looks from and where bought, taken back and test drive vehicles come out.

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Preview spot | Stand where the shown vehicle should be, facing its front direction. |
| Camera | Look at the preview spot with the game camera and use the camera position. |
| Delivery spot | A free place on the road, facing the way out. It may be the preview spot. |

**NPC** card: an optional person at the counter (**Model**, **What it does**, **Where it stands**). Leave the model empty for none.

### Tab: Settings

Prices, the lot's cut, payment and test drives.

**Sales**: what sellers may ask and how much the lot keeps.

| Job | The job that gets the cut. Shown when "A job" is picked. | | empty |
| Gang | The gang that gets the cut. Shown when "A gang" is picked. | | empty |
| Into its account | The job's or gang's shared account the cut is paid into. | | bank |

## Commands and keybinds

None. The prompts use the interact key (**E** by default). **ESC** closes the screen.

## Logs

Two log channels: `listings` (a vehicle listed or taken back) and `sales` (sales, dealer buy-backs, payouts received at login, and a lot's cut that could not be paid).

## Known limits

- Only owned vehicles of your own character can be sold (not job or gang fleet vehicles, not rentals).
- Listing saves the vehicle's damage and fuel, but never its tuning: the stored look stays as it was.
- The dealer's money comes from nowhere (as in QB): it is new money in the economy. Set **Dealer pays** to 0 to turn it off.
- There is no paper contract between two players: sales go only through a lot.
