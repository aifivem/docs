ai-banking is the bank screen of AI Core. At a bank branch players deposit, withdraw, transfer money to other players and read their transaction history; bosses also manage their job's account. ATMs pay out and transfer. ai-banking keeps no money of its own: every balance is ai-core's, so money is the same everywhere.

## What it does in the game

- **Bank branch.** Walk to a branch counter and open the bank. Tabs: the card and balance, accounts, activity, and the forms to deposit, withdraw and transfer.
- **ATM.** Use any ATM prop (the models in the panel). ATMs only withdraw and transfer, within the ATM limits.
- **Transfers.** To a player who is online, by server id or citizen id. The panel's transfer fee (a percent) is paid by the sender on top.
- **History.** Every money change of the account, newest first, read from ai-core's ledger, page by page.
- **Society accounts.** A player whose job grade is a boss grade sees that job's `bank` account and can deposit and withdraw. Every boss with the screen open sees changes at once.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-banking` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs `ai-core`.

It reads ai-core's account and transaction tables, so it needs its own database permission line in `env.cfg`:

```ini title="env.cfg"
add_convar_permission ai-banking read mysql_connection_string
```

The recipe adds this line. In ai-core's panel, **General > Bank** picks the banking script; `auto` uses ai-banking first.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Banking**. Defaults come from `data/banks.json` and `data/settings.json`.

### Banks tab

One record per branch. Defaults: six Fleeca branches, Pacific Standard and Blaine County Savings (Paleto Bay).

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for a new bank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The branch name, shown on the blip. 1-48 characters. | | Fleeca Bank |
| Counter | Where players open the bank. Stand at the counter and use your position. | | 0, 0, 0 |
| Reach | How close the player must be to the counter. 1-20. | m | 3 |
| Map blip | Shows the branch on the map. | | on |
| Blip look | Sprite, colour and size (shown when **Map blip** is on). | | sprite 108, colour 2, size 0.7 |

Each branch also has an **NPC** card: **Model** (empty means no NPC), **What it does** (idle animation) and **Where it stands**.

### Rules tab

ATMs pay out and transfer; deposits and society accounts need a bank.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ATM models | World props that work as ATMs. Up to 20 model names. | | `prop_atm_01`, `prop_atm_02`, `prop_atm_03`, `prop_fleeca_atm` |
| ATM limit per action | Most one ATM withdrawal or transfer moves. Bank branches have no limit. 0-10,000,000. | $ | 5,000 |
| ATM limit per day | Most one character withdraws and transfers through ATMs in 24 hours. Counted in memory: a restart of ai-banking starts every count again. 0-100,000,000. | $ | 20,000 |
| Transfer fee | Percent added to transfers to other players (0 = free). 0-50, in steps of 0.5. | % | 0 |

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

None. Branches and ATMs use ai-core's **Interaction style**: a prompt with **E**, or a target option.

## What it stands in for

`fxProvide`: **qb-banking** and **Renewed-Banking**. Scripts that use their society money exports reach ai-banking:

- `exports['qb-banking']:GetAccountBalance(job)`, `AddMoney(job, amount, reason)`, `RemoveMoney(job, amount, reason)`
- `exports['Renewed-Banking']:getAccountMoney(job)`, `addAccountMoney(job, amount, reason)`, `removeAccountMoney(job, amount, reason)`

The account is a job name; the money is ai-core's job `bank` account.

## Other banking scripts

AI Core's banking layer also supports tgiann-bank, Renewed-Banking, qb-banking, okokBankingV2, fd_banking, wasabi_banking, kartik-banking and tgg-banking. Pick one in ai-core's panel (**General > Bank**) and stop ai-banking. See [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility).

## Known limits

- Transfers go only to players who are online, because ai-core keeps money in memory for online characters.
- The server cannot see ATM props: it trusts the ATM position the player's game sends and checks the player stays within 3 m of it. That is why ATMs have their own limits; branches have none.
- Log channels: `transfers` and `society`.
