ai-cityhall runs the city hall counter. In the job center players take a public job (taxi, trucker, garbage...) at its start grade. At the documents desk they buy an ID card or a driver license. Using a document shows the card, with the holder's photo, on the screen of everyone standing next to them.

## What it does in the game

- **Counter.** Walk to a city hall counter and open the menu (ai-lib's menu): **Job center** and **Documents**.
- **Job center.** Lists the panel's public jobs with their names from ai-core. Picking one sets the player's job at the panel's grade. Jobs that do not exist in ai-core are skipped.
- **Documents.** Buy a document item for its price, paid from the panel's accounts in order. The item carries the holder's first and last name, birth date, sex, nationality and citizen id.
- **Showing a document.** Using the item plays a short showing animation and puts the card on the screen of everyone within 3 m, the holder included, for the panel's time. You keep the item. The photo is the holder's face, made live on each viewer's game; a card shown by someone who is not its holder has no photo.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-cityhall` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs `ai-core`.

The documents are the items `id_card` and `driver_license` (ai-inventory has them in its default item list). With another inventory, add these items as unique (not stackable) items. A document item added in the panel works at once.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **City hall**. Defaults come from `data/halls.json` and `data/settings.json`.

### City halls tab

One record per city hall. Default: one City Hall with the NPC `a_m_m_hasjew_01`.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default for a new hall |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The hall's name, shown on the blip. 1-48 characters. | | City Hall |
| Counter | Where players open the menu. Stand at the counter and use your position. | | 0, 0, 0 |
| Reach | How close the player must be to the counter. 1-20. | m | 2.5 |
| Map blip | Shows the hall on the map. | | on |
| Blip look | Sprite, colour and size (shown when **Map blip** is on). | | sprite 419, colour 0, size 0.7 |

Each hall also has an **NPC** card: **Model** (empty means no NPC), **What it does** (idle animation) and **Where it stands**.

### Offers tab

This file stays on the server; players get the offer when they open the counter.

**Job center**: public jobs and the grade a new member starts at.

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jobs | Up to 30 rows, each a **Job** (its name in ai-core) and a **Grade** (0-50) the player starts at. | trucker, taxi, tow, reporter, garbage, bus, hotdog, all at grade 0 |

**Documents**: items players buy here.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Documents | Up to 20 rows, each an **Item** and a **Price** (0-1,000,000). | $ | `id_card` $50, `driver_license` $250 |
| Paid from | Money accounts tried in order. Up to 5. | | cash, bank |
| Shown document stays | Seconds a document stays on the screens of the players it was shown to (everyone within 3 m and the holder). 2-60. | s | 8 |

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

None. The counter uses ai-core's **Interaction style** (prompt with **E**, or a target option).

## What it stands in for

No `fxProvide`. It does the work of qb-cityhall (the default job list is qb-cityhall's) and qbx_idcard (the document item fields are qbx_idcard's), without their exports or events.

## Known limits

- A document removed from the panel stays registered as a usable item until the next restart; using it does nothing.
- Qbox's stored mugshot image is not used: the photo is made live from the holder's face.
- Log channel: `cityhall`.
