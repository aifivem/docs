ai-jobs gives players simple jobs they can start at a depot: drive a route and get paid per stop, tow broken-down cars, or gather items on foot. Next to the routes it runs a hot dog stand: rent a cart, push it around, cook and sell to passers-by. Every route and every stop is set in the admin panel, so you can add new jobs without writing code.

## What it does in the game

- **Starting a shift.** Each route has a depot with a map blip. Walk to the depot and use the point (**Start working**). Driving routes put a work vehicle at the route's spawn point and give you its keys. Use the depot again (**Stop working**) to end the shift.
- **Stops.** The server picks your next stop and shows it on the map. At the stop, use the point (**Work here**): a progress bar runs for the route's time per stop, then you are paid (or get items on a gathering route) and the next stop appears.
- **Route kinds.**
  - **Random stops**: the next stop is any other stop of the route (taxi, trucker, news, garbage).
  - **Stops in order**: the stops follow each other by their id and start again from the first (bus).
  - **Gathering on foot**: no vehicle; each stop gives a random number of an item (recycling, vineyard, diving). Underwater stops play a diving animation.
  - **Towing**: a locked broken-down car waits at the stop. Load it onto the flatbed, then the depot becomes the next stop. You are paid at the depot, only if the towed car is still within 15 m of the flatbed.
- **Shift end.** A shift ends when the stops per shift are done (0 means it never ends by itself), when you stop it at the depot, when you leave the server, or when the resource stops. The work vehicle and a towed car are deleted then. Nothing is saved in the database.
- **Pay.** Driving and tow routes pay the route's pay per stop into the route's pay account (bank by default).

### Hot dog stand

1. At the counter, rent a stand. The deposit is taken from the deposit account. The stand appears at its spawn point next to the counter.
2. Use the stand to open its menu: **Push the stand**, **Prepare hot dogs**, **Start selling** / **Stop selling**.
3. While pushing, press **G** to let go. You also let go when you fall, die, aim, fight or get into a vehicle.
4. **Prepare hot dogs** runs a row of skill checks. No miss makes a perfect (A) hot dog, one miss a rare (B) one, more misses a common (C) one. Each hot dog raises your hot dog reputation. From level 2 on, half of the batches are lucky and give more than one hot dog.
5. **Start selling**: nearby passers-by walk up to the stand and make an offer (1 to 3 hot dogs of one quality, at a price from your level's range). Accept it at the customer. A customer waits 30 s. Walking more than 10 m from the stand or running out of stock stops selling.
6. Give the stand back at the counter to get the deposit back. Logging out with a stand loses the deposit; a resource stop refunds it.

Reputation is stored on the character (`rep.hotdog`, the same key QBCore uses), so it stays between sessions. Your level is the highest level whose **From reputation** you reached; the level sets how many hot dogs of each quality the stand holds and their prices.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: put the folder in `resources/[ai]/` and add `ensure ai-jobs` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs `ai-core` and OneSync.

The gathering routes give `scrapmetal`, `grape` and `coral` by default. Make sure these items exist in your inventory's item list, or change the route's item in the panel. The default work vehicles are GTA models (`taxi`, `bus`, `rumpo`, `trash2`, `flatbed`), no extra vehicles are needed.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Work routes**. It has three tabs: **Routes**, **Stops** and **Hot dog stand**. Defaults come from `data/routes.json`, `data/stops.json` and `data/hotdog.json`.

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

Each player can change these in GTA's key settings (FiveM section); the owner changes the defaults in the admin panel (**Keybinds**).

| Default key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| G | Let go of the hot dog stand you are pushing (`jobs_hotdog_release`). |

Depots, stops, the counter and the stand are interaction points: a prompt with the interaction key (**E** by default) or a target option, depending on ai-core's **Interaction style** setting.

## What it stands in for

No `fxProvide`. One engine covers what QBCore / Qbox ship as separate job scripts (taxi, bus, trucker, garbage, tow, news and gathering jobs), and the hot dog stand is a port of qb-hotdogjob. It does not answer those scripts' exports or events.

## Compatible scripts

Work vehicle keys go through AI Core's key layer, items through the inventory layer. Any key script and inventory AI Core supports works (see [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility)).

## Known limits

- A shift is kept in memory only: leaving the server or restarting the resource ends it, and the work vehicle is deleted.
- The server refuses a stop when the player is more than 12 m from it, more than 15 m from the work vehicle (driving routes), or arrives faster than 60 m/s from the last stop (teleports).
- Hot dogs are not inventory items: stock lives on the stand, and customers are NPCs only. Players cannot buy from each other.
- Log channels: `work` (route pay) and `hotdog` (sales).
