ai-streetraces lets players start a street race anywhere. A driver sets a waypoint on the map as the finish and picks a buy-in. Drivers nearby get an invitation. After a countdown, the first racer the server sees at the finish takes every buy-in.

## What it does in the game

1. **Create.** Set a waypoint at least the panel's **Shortest race** away. In a vehicle, open the radial menu and pick **Street race** (or type `/race`), then **Race to my waypoint**, and type the buy-in. The buy-in is taken from your money account.
2. **Invite.** Drivers within the **Invitation distance** get an invitation. They open the same menu and pick **Join (name)'s race** within the join time. Joining needs the driver seat and the start line nearby; the buy-in is taken on join.
3. **Start.** When the join time ends (or the host picks **Start now** once two racers are in), racers who are not at the start line in a vehicle are sent out with their buy-in back. With fewer than two racers the race is off and everyone gets their money back.
4. **Countdown.** A 5 second countdown; vehicles are frozen. Then **Go!**
5. **Finish.** The finish has a blip and a marker. The first racer the server sees there in a vehicle takes the whole pot.
6. **Time limit.** If nobody finishes within the panel's **Time limit**, the pot is split evenly between the racers still in.

**Leaving.** Before the start, leaving (or the host cancelling) refunds the buy-in. After the start, giving up or leaving the server loses the buy-in into the pot.

**Police.** When the countdown starts, the race may alert the police at the start line (panel **Alert chance**).

**Cheating.** The server checks each racer's position every 2 seconds. Moving faster than any car can (a teleport) disqualifies the racer; their buy-in stays in the pot. A finish faster than the straight line at 100 m/s is not counted.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-streetraces` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs `ai-core`. No database table, no items.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Street races** > tab **Settings**. Defaults come from `data/settings.json`. Changes apply to races created after the change.

**Money**: racers pay the buy-in when they join; the first at the finish takes every buy-in.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Money account | The account buy-ins are taken from and prizes and refunds are paid to. | | cash |
| Lowest buy-in | The smallest buy-in a player may set; 0 allows free races. 0-10,000,000. | $ | 100 |
| Highest buy-in | The largest buy-in a player may set; not below the lowest buy-in. 0-10,000,000. | $ | 15,000 |

**Races**

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Join time | How long invited drivers have to join; the host can start earlier. A 5 second countdown follows. 15-300. | s | 45 |
| Racers per race | The most racers one race takes, the host included. 2-16. | | 8 |
| Invitation distance | Drivers this close to the host get the invitation; racers must stay this close (plus 25 m) to the start. 10-400. | m | 60 |
| Shortest race | The straight-line distance from the start to the waypoint must be at least this. 100-10,000. | m | 500 |
| Time limit | A race nobody finishes in this time ends and the pot is split between the racers still in it. 1-60. | min | 10 |

**Police**: alerts go through the alert system ai-core uses.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alert chance | The chance that a race start sends a police alert at the start line; 0 never alerts. 0-100. | % | 25 |

## Commands

| Command | Arguments | Who may use it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/race` | none | everyone | Opens the street race menu (create, join, start, leave). The same menu as the radial item, for servers without a radial menu. |

## Keybinds

None. The menu opens from the radial menu (**Street race**, shown while driving, invited or racing) or with `/race`.

## What it stands in for

No `fxProvide`. It replaces qb-streetraces / qbx_streetraces (one menu instead of their four commands), without their exports or events.

## Compatible scripts

Money goes through ai-core, alerts through the dispatch layer, the menu item through the radial layer. Any alert script and radial menu AI Core supports works (see [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility)).

## Known limits

- Point-to-point races only: no laps, no saved tracks, no checkpoints.
- The finish has no height: it is a flat circle around the waypoint.
- Buy-ins of racers who all left a running race are lost (written to the log).
- A resource stop refunds each remaining racer their buy-in.
- Log channel: `races`.
