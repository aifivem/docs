ai-drugs gives players two ways to make money with drugs: selling to strangers on the street, and growing weed. The server decides every sale (price, refusal, police alert), and plants are saved in the database, so they keep growing through restarts.

## What it does in the game

### Street sales

1. Open the radial menu and pick **Sell drugs** to start looking for buyers. Pick it again to stop.
2. While selling, a stranger from nearby walks up to you. A **Sell to the stranger** prompt appears when they stand next to you.
3. The server picks one drug you carry from the panel's list, a count and a price in that drug's range. The buyer may refuse (**Refuse chance**). The money goes to the panel's money account.
4. A sale may alert the police (**Police alert chance**). Nobody buys while fewer than **Police needed** officers are on duty.

A player cannot sell more often than **Time between sales**, and the same stranger does not buy again for 10 minutes.

### Weed plants

1. Use a `weed_seed` outside to plant a weed plant. Plants cannot stand within 2 m of each other, and one character can have at most **Plants per character** plants.
2. The plant grows over **Growing time**: a young prop, a growing prop from 40 %, a ready prop at the end. The prompt shows the growth in percent.
3. Anyone can harvest a ready plant: a progress bar, then a random number of `weed_bud` between **Buds from** and **Buds up to**.
4. On-duty police can destroy a growing plant.
5. A ready plant that nobody harvests disappears after **Rots after**.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-drugs` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs `ai-core` and OneSync.

- It stores plants in its own table `ai_drugs_plants`, created at start. It needs its database permission line in `env.cfg`:

```ini title="env.cfg"
add_convar_permission ai-drugs read mysql_connection_string
```

- Items used by the defaults: `weed_seed` (usable), `weed_bud`, `coke_baggy`, `meth_baggy`. ai-inventory has them; with another inventory, add them or change the panel.
- The **Sell drugs** item shows on whichever radial menu AI Core supports.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Drugs**. Defaults come from `data/settings.json` and `data/plants.json`. The **Street sales** and **Weed plants** settings stay on the server.

### Street sales tab

Players sell to strangers after turning selling on in the radial menu.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drugs | Up to 30 rows, each an **Item** with **Price from** and **Price up to** (0-1,000,000). A sale is priced between the two. | $ | `weed_bud` $40-80, `coke_baggy` $90-160, `meth_baggy` $120-200 |
| Time between sales | One player cannot sell more often than this. 1-600. | s | 10 |
| Refuse chance | Chance a buyer says "Not interested". 0-100. | % | 20 |
| Police alert chance | Chance that a sale alerts on-duty police. 0-100. | % | 20 |
| Police needed | On-duty officers needed before anyone buys. 0-32. | | 0 |
| Police jobs | On-duty members count as police and can destroy plants. Up to 10. | | police |
| Money account | Sale money goes to this account. | | cash |

### Weed plants tab

Anyone can harvest a ready plant; police destroy growing ones.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Growing time | How long a plant takes to be ready. 1-10,080. | min | 60 |
| Rots after | A ready plant nobody harvests disappears after this long. 1-10,080. | min | 120 |
| Plants per character | How many plants one character can have at once. 0-100. | | 5 |
| Buds from | Fewest buds per harvest. 1-100. | | 3 |
| Buds up to | Most buds per harvest. 1-100. | | 8 |
| Seed item | Using this item plants a weed plant. A new item works at once. | | weed_seed |
| Harvest item | A ready plant gives this item. | | weed_bud |

### Plant look tab

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

None. Selling starts from the radial menu (**Sell drugs**). Buyers and plants use prompts (**E** by default).

## What it stands in for

No `fxProvide`. It does not answer another drug script's exports or events.

## Compatible scripts

Items go through AI Core's inventory layer, police alerts through the dispatch layer, the sell switch through the radial layer. Any inventory, alert script and radial menu AI Core supports works (see [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility)).

## Known limits

- Only weed grows; coke and meth are sold, not made.
- Growth is time only: there is no water or fertilizer.
- Log channel: `drugs`.
