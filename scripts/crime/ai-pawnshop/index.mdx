ai-pawnshop gives players a place to turn loot into money and stolen cars into materials. Pawnshops buy items at the prices you set. Scrapyards take apart vehicles that nobody owns and give materials for them.

## What it does in the game

- **Pawnshop.** Walk to the counter and open the shop. The menu lists the items this shop buys that you carry, with the price per piece. Pick one, type how many, and get paid into the panel's money account.
- **Scrapyard.** Park a vehicle within 12 m of the yard's centre, get out and use the yard point. Nobody may sit in the driver seat. A progress bar runs (the panel's time per vehicle); then the vehicle is deleted and you get a random count of each material.
- **Only stolen vehicles.** The yard refuses vehicles the server created (rentals, job and service cars, owned cars, the armored truck) and vehicles registered in ai-vehicles: "The yard does not take registered vehicles".
- **Scrap wait.** After a vehicle, the same character waits the panel's time before the next one. A reconnect does not reset it.

## Installation

Installed by the AI Core recipe. By hand: `ensure ai-pawnshop` after `ensure ai-core`. It needs `ai-core`.

Items used by the defaults: `goldchain`, `diamond_ring`, `rolex`, `coral` (bought), `scrapmetal`, `plastic`, `copper`, `steel`, `glass` (scrap materials). ai-inventory has them; with another inventory, add them or change the panel.

## Panel settings

Admin panel > **Pawnshops**. Defaults come from `data/shops.json`, `data/scrapyards.json` and `data/settings.json`.

### Pawnshops tab

One record per shop. Defaults: Vinewood Pawn & Jewelry (buys `goldchain`, `diamond_ring`, `rolex` at $75 each) and Del Perro Diving Shop (buys `coral` at $75, NPC `a_m_m_salton_01`).

**Pawnshop**

| Setting | What it changes | Default for a new shop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The shop's name, shown on the blip. 1-48 characters. | empty |
| Place | Stand at the counter and use your position. | 0, 0, 0 |
| Show on the map | Shows the shop's blip. | on |
| Blip look | Sprite, colour and size. | sprite 431, colour 5, size 0.7 |

**What it buys**

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Items | Up to 100 rows, each an **Item** and the **Price** paid per piece (0-10,000,000). | $ | empty |

Each shop also has an **NPC** card: **Model** (empty means no NPC), **What it does** (idle animation) and **Where it stands**.

### Scrapyards tab

One record per yard. Default: Sandy Shores Scrap Yard.

**Scrapyard**

| Setting | What it changes | Default for a new yard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | The yard's name, shown on the blip. 1-48 characters. | empty |
| Place | The middle of the yard; vehicles within 12 m count. | 0, 0, 0 |
| Show on the map | Shows the yard's blip. | on |
| Blip look | Sprite, colour and size. | sprite 380, colour 9, size 0.7 |

**Materials** (what one vehicle gives)

| Setting | What it changes | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Materials | Up to 20 rows, each an **Item** with **From** and **Up to** (0-100). Each vehicle gives a random count between the two. | Sandy Shores: `scrapmetal` 4-10, `plastic` 2-6, `copper` 1-4, `steel` 1-4, `glass` 1-4 |

### Settings tab

Used by every pawnshop and scrapyard. These settings stay on the server.

| Setting | What it changes | Unit | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Money account | Sales are paid into this account. | | cash |
| Time per vehicle | How long scrapping one vehicle takes. 1-600. | s | 30 |
| Wait between vehicles | How long one player waits before scrapping the next vehicle. 0-1440. | min | 15 |

## Commands

None.

## Keybinds

None. Shops and yards use ai-core's **Interaction style** (prompt with **E**, or a target option).

## What it stands in for

No `fxProvide`. It does not answer another pawnshop or scrapyard script's exports or events.

## Compatible scripts

Items go through AI Core's inventory layer, so any inventory AI Core supports works. The owned-vehicle check reads ai-vehicles (see [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility)).

## Known limits

- Shops only buy; they do not sell items to players.
- Log channels: `pawn` (sales) and `scrap` (scrapped vehicles).
