---
title: Crime and racing
description: Robberies, drugs, pawnshops, scrapyards and street races.
icon: Siren
---

The crime scripts give players illegal ways to earn money. Every step is checked on the server, and police alerts go through AI Core, so they reach whichever police alert script you run.

| Script | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [ai-robberies](/docs/scripts/crime/ai-robberies) | Store registers and safes, Fleeca banks, Vangelico, house robberies and the armored truck. |
| [ai-drugs](/docs/scripts/crime/ai-drugs) | Street sales to strangers and weed plants that grow over time. |
| [ai-pawnshop](/docs/scripts/crime/ai-pawnshop) | Pawnshops buying loot and scrapyards taking stolen vehicles apart. |
| [ai-streetraces](/docs/scripts/crime/ai-streetraces) | Street races to a waypoint with a buy-in. |
