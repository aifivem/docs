Every member of `aiLib`, from ai-lib's `init.lua`. Most are the twin of a TypeScript function and are documented on that function's page, with the Lua call in the signature block and a Lua example tab. **Waits** means the call pauses until the answer comes: call it inside a thread (see [Functions that wait](/docs/developers/api/lua#functions-that-wait)).

Methods of a class (`points:add(...)`, `job:set(...)`) are listed with their class.
